# Google Play listing — English

## Title
BRNV Currency Converter

## Short description
Currency and crypto conversion with multiple currencies and home screen widgets.

## Full description
BRNV Currency Converter is a minimalist currency converter for travel, work and everyday calculations.

Add your currencies and enter an amount in any row. The other values update automatically. No sign-up, subscriptions or ads.

MULTIPLE CURRENCIES AT ONCE
Compare amounts in several currencies on one screen. Add, replace, remove and reorder currencies. Search by name or code and save your favorites.

RATES ON YOUR HOME SCREEN
Set up a widget with one or two currency pairs. Choose amounts, toggle flags and resize your widget. Each widget keeps its own settings.

CURRENCIES AND CRYPTO
Convert traditional currencies and supported cryptocurrencies in the same app.

KEEP CONVERTING OFFLINE
After a successful download, rates are saved on your device. If your connection is temporarily unavailable, continue calculating with the last saved rates.

ONLY WHAT YOU NEED
Light and dark themes, device language support and manual language selection. Settings and favorites stay on your device. Every feature is free.

Rates are indicative and may differ from those offered by banks and exchange services. The app does not exchange money, process transfers or provide trading services. An internet connection is required to download new rates.
